Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 • ©sql.ssl 2026

We at Fahad Asi Manea Al-Zaferi Co respect your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

1. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery location (address and map point), and the products you order.
  • Payment proof: if you pay by WAMD, you may upload a payment screenshot so the shop can verify your payment.
  • Basket (cart): saved locally in your own browser so your selected products stay when you move between pages.
  • Basic visit statistics: anonymous visitor IDs and timestamps to count how many people visit the shop website.
  • AI assistant messages: the text you type and any photo you send, only to find products for you.

2. How it is used

  • To prepare and deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To verify WAMD payments (screenshot is seen only by the shop admin).
  • To show the shop owner live sales, orders and visitor statistics.
  • To answer your questions in the AI assistant and search products (including from photos you send).

3. Sharing

When you press Submit Order, your order details are sent to the shop's WhatsApp so the shop can process it. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or any other third parties.

4. Where data is stored

Order data is stored on secure Google Firebase servers and payment screenshots on Cloudinary. Only the shop administrator can open the admin panel and view orders.

5. Cookies & local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage for your basket, language preference of the translate widget, and your visitor ID. You can clear them anytime from your browser settings.
